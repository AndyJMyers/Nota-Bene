NOTA BENE

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 September 2026

Nota Bene is a personal record-keeping Android app developed and published by Andy J Myers. Its records stay on your device unless you deliberately export them.

Records you choose to keep

Nota Bene can store card-payment details; medicines, schedules, dose history and stock; symptoms and measurements; tasks and dependencies; research questions and notes; and associated dates, times and completion states. This may include personal, financial and health information. You decide what to enter and accept.

These records are stored in Nota Bene’s local database on your device. There is no Nota Bene account or developer-operated server. The developer cannot see your records. Nota Bene does not sell them, use them for advertising or transmit them to the developer.

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of Nota Bene app data are disabled. Losing, clearing or replacing the phone without an explicit export may therefore lose the records.

Receipt text recognition

When you select a receipt image, bundled Google ML Kit software recognises its text on-device so Nota Bene can propose editable payment details. Nota Bene does not retain the receipt image. The receipt image and recognised text are not sent to Google’s servers by this bundled text-recognition feature.

ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information, including device and app information, performance metrics, a per-installation identifier and feature event/configuration metadata. This is not your receipt image, recognised text or Nota Bene database. Google’s handling is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Speech input

When you choose speech input, Nota Bene launches the speech-recognition service installed on your phone. That service may process audio under its provider’s settings and privacy policy. Nota Bene does not retain audio; it keeps only text you accept. Keyboard entry remains available.

MEDS reminders

Reminder checks compare user-entered schedules and dose records locally. No medicine information is sent to a reminder server. Android may delay or suppress notifications because of battery management, permissions or device settings. Reminders are a helpful aid, not a guaranteed service or a substitute for prescriptions and professional medical advice.

Medicine details in notifications are marked private. Nota Bene supplies a generic public version for a locked screen, while Android and your lock-screen settings make the final display decision. Protect the phone with an appropriate screen lock.

Explicit export

The Export XLSX control opens Android’s save picker and creates a workbook only when you request it. You choose where to save it. A cloud-storage destination may upload and process the workbook under that provider’s terms. Protect exported workbooks as sensitive data.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until you delete them, use Erase all local data in Settings, clear Nota Bene storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Erase all local data removes Nota Bene records and reminder-state markers on the device. Previously exported copies must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Nota Bene has no account, so there is no separate account-deletion process. Google or a speech provider may retain its own limited service data under its published policy.

Security

Nota Bene relies on Android’s application sandbox and the security of your phone. Its local database is not separately encrypted by Nota Bene. No storage or security mechanism can be guaranteed completely secure.

Medical limits

Nota Bene is a personal recording and organisation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition, calculate doses or recommend treatment. Do not use it as your only essential reminder. Seek professional advice for medical questions and use emergency services when necessary.

The MEDS colour indicator compares the number of entries recorded today only with the usual daily count entered by the user. It is not a medically derived safety or overdose threshold.

Children

Nota Bene is intended for adults and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s information through a developer-operated service.

Changes

This policy will be updated when Nota Bene’s functionality or data handling changes. The effective date above will be revised.
