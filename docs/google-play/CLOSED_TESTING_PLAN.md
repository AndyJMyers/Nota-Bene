Checked 5 September 2026. No sale or closed release was created during this check; Nota Bene remains paid at the agreed UK customer price of GBP 4.99.

## The 32 Visualisations precedent

Read the prior development conversation and verified the actual Play Console sale, not merely the original plan. The ended sale named **Tester free 14 days** actually ran **17–24 August 2026**. Its discount type was **Fixed price**, with zero sale prices. The base app remained Paid. The previous conversation records difficulties with promo codes and a rejected 100% percentage discount; fixed-price zero was the working route.

## Recommended Nota Bene sequence

1. Prepare and submit the closed-testing release and completed listing/declarations for review.
2. Once the closed release is available and testers are ready, create a temporary **Fixed price = 0** sale for every targeted country. Verify every local sale price is zero before saving; do not use **Make your app free**.
3. Send the closed-track opt-in/install instructions when free acquisition is available. Verify a tester can acquire it without payment. Existing internal testers must leave internal testing before joining the closed track.
4. Ensure at least 12 testers remain opted in continuously for 14 days and genuinely test the app. The acquisition sale window and the testing period are different clocks; the sale ending does not turn an already acquired copy into a subscription.
5. Collect feedback, address material issues, and apply for production access when Console enables it.

Google currently documents a 1–14-day sale duration and a 30-day interval between sales, and requires the app to be published to create/edit a sale. The live Console imposed a shorter interval in our earlier 32 V setup; use the actual permitted dates when scheduling. Do not start a sale while awaiting review or tester readiness.

A separate free package would add maintenance and would not qualify testing of the paid package. Internal testing is free for paid apps, but does not replace the required closed test. Promo codes remain an alternative, not the preferred path after our earlier experience.
