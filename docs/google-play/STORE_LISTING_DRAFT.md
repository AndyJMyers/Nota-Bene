## Full description

Nota Bene is a small personal organiser for the things you want to keep track of.

Start with a simple TODO list, then add the collections that suit you: a running log, a record of something worth retaining, or a repeatable item with an optional interval, stock count and reminder.

Choose the quickest input at the time. Type, speak, or take a photo / choose an image to extract text that you can edit before keeping.

Your records stay on your phone. Nota Bene has no account, no developer-operated server and no advertising. Export an XLSX workbook only when you choose to make a copy.

Features:

- Create and name your own TODO, LOG, RECORD and REPEAT collections.
- Keep notes, dates, quantities, references and completion events.
- Record repeat events and view their history.
- Choose optional, device-based reminders for repeat items.
- Use system speech recognition and on-device image-text capture.
- Export your collections to an XLSX workbook.
- Choose from seven atmospheric visual treatments without changing how the app works.

Nota Bene is a neutral tool: you decide the meaning of every collection and record.
