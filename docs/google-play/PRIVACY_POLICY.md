# Privacy Policy for Nota Bene

Effective date: **[INSERT PUBLICATION DATE]**

Nota Bene is a personal record-keeping Android application developed and published by **[INSERT PLAY STORE DEVELOPER NAME]**. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Information handled by the app

Nota Bene can store information that the user deliberately enters or accepts, including:

- card-payment details such as merchant, amount and notes;
- medicines, dosages, dose schedules, dose history and stock information;
- symptoms, health observations and measurements;
- tasks, dependencies, research questions and completion states; and
- dates and times associated with those records.

This information may include personal, financial and health information. The user chooses what to enter.

## Storage and use

Nota Bene stores its primary records in a local database on the user’s device. The developer does not operate a Nota Bene account, server, advertising network or analytics service, and the app does not send these records to the developer.

The information is used only to provide the app’s recording, display, dose-status and export functions.

Android may include app data in device or account backups when the user has enabled the operating system’s backup facilities. Those backups are controlled by the user’s device, Android version and account provider, not by the Nota Bene developer.

## Speech input

When the user chooses speech input, Nota Bene launches the speech-recognition service installed on the device and receives the resulting text. Audio processing is performed by that external system service and may be governed by the service provider’s own settings and privacy policy. Nota Bene does not retain the audio recording.

Users can enter the same information with the keyboard instead.

## Receipt images and text recognition

When the user selects a receipt image, Nota Bene uses on-device text recognition to propose editable payment details. Nota Bene does not keep a permanent copy of the receipt image. The accepted text is stored only when the user chooses to save it.

## Export and sharing

The export control creates an XLSX workbook through Android’s standard document picker. The user chooses its destination. A destination such as Google Drive, Dropbox or another cloud provider may upload and process the exported file under that provider’s terms and privacy policy.

Nota Bene does not otherwise sell, rent or share the user’s records with third parties.

## Security

Nota Bene relies on Android’s application sandbox and local database storage. Users should protect their device with an appropriate screen lock and treat exported workbooks as sensitive, particularly when they contain financial or health information.

No storage or security mechanism can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain on the device until the user deletes them using an available in-app control, clears Nota Bene’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalls the app. Some record types do not yet have individual deletion controls; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local database.

Copies previously exported by the user and copies retained in an Android or third-party backup must be deleted separately from the location in which they were saved.

Nota Bene has no user accounts, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

## Children

Nota Bene is intended for adults and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Medical disclaimer

Nota Bene is a personal recording and organisation tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Users should contact an appropriate healthcare professional about medical questions and use emergency services when necessary.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Nota Bene’s functionality or data handling changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published.
