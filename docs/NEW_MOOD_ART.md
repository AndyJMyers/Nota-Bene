# Four new mood backgrounds

These project assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool for the Nota Bene Android app. Each is a portrait background with a quiet centre so controls and entries remain readable. The user interface ornament is drawn in Compose.

## Slate

Asset: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/slate_field.png`

Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen portrait background artwork for an Android personal notebook app theme called SLATE. A commanding but quiet composition of rain-dark Welsh slate slabs, layered cleavage planes, fine silver mineral veins, beads and rivulets of water, a tiny restrained warm-gold glint. Real slate, graphite and wet stone, almost-black charcoal with cold blue-grey. Portrait 2:3, subtle detail concentrated at edges and top/bottom, broad dark low-contrast central area kept legible behind app controls and cards. Sophisticated tactile illustration, natural irregularity. No text, typography, logos, icons, controls, borders, watermark, people, or buildings.
